Process at a glance

  1. Confirm DHSUD authority, ELECOM constitution, bylaws and membership list.
  2. Issue Form 1 to all eligible members; record qualifying receipts on Form 2.
  3. Verify unique eligible recipients and calculate quorum; do not confuse receipts with votes.
  4. Issue separate anonymous Form 3 ballots at the authorized election and record actual turnout.
  5. Preserve delivery logs, proxies, ballots, canvass, minutes and election report for DHSUD.

Reference for review: DHSUD Department Circular No. 2024-018, Rule XIII, Sections 89–93; DHSUD Interim MC No. 2025-003. Verify exact procedures and any updated instructions with DHSUD.
